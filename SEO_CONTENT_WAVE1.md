# Snay3i.ma — SEO Content Wave 1

## Goal
Build useful, search-intent pages around Moroccan home-service needs without thin doorway pages. Every page must offer genuinely useful local information and a clear path to finding a professional.

## Priority service clusters
1. Plomberie
2. Électricité
3. Serrurerie
4. Peinture
5. Carrelage
6. Climatisation
7. Maçonnerie
8. Menuiserie

## Priority cities
Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech, Tanger, Agadir, Fès, Oujda, Kénitra.

## First page briefs
### Plombier à Casablanca

Sections: common plumbing emergencies; what information to send before requesting help; questions to ask about diagnosis/quote; emergency vs planned work; how to compare profiles; Snay3i CTA.

## Internal linking rules
- Service pages link to relevant city/service pages where a real page exists.
- City pages link to multiple relevant services and one useful guide.
- Guides link back to the most relevant service page.
- Avoid generating hundreds of near-identical city/service pages solely for rankings.

## Content quality rules
- Write for customers first, not search engines.
- Do not fabricate prices, ratings, reviews, certifications or availability.
- Use specific Moroccan context only where accurate.
- Add original practical advice and clear next steps.
- One primary CTA per page.

## CTA
Customer: “Trouvez un professionnel sur Snay3i.ma.”
Professional: “Ajoutez votre activité sur Snay3i.ma.”

## Measurement
Track organic visits, landing-page engagement, profile views, contact/WhatsApp/phone clicks and professional sign-ups. Success is qualified enquiries, not page count.
