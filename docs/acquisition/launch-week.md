# Snay3i.ma — Launch Week Free Traffic Playbook

## Goal

Generate the first repeatable flow of artisan sign-ups and customer leads without paid advertising and without changing the submitted AdSense application.

## Rules

- Never claim an artisan is verified unless the platform has verified that artisan.
- Never invent ratings, reviews, availability, prices, customer counts, or coverage.
- Use a tracked URL for every campaign.
- Send artisans to `/rejoindre`.
- Send customers to a relevant service/city page whenever one exists.

## Comment/community strategy

- Answer local Facebook-group questions when allowed by group rules.
- Do not spam links.
- Give one useful answer first, then include the relevant Snay3i page when it genuinely helps.
- Use the city + métier language people actually use in the post.

## Weekly metrics

Track:

- `/rejoindre` visits
- artisan registration starts
- artisan registrations completed
- service/city page views
- profile opens
- phone clicks
- WhatsApp clicks
- `lead_conversion` events
- top source / medium / campaign combinations

## Success criterion

Do not judge the week by follower count. Judge it by:

**qualified artisan sign-ups + customer contact actions + lead conversions.**
