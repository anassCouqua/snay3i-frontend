# Snay3i.ma — 30-Day Organic Growth Playbook

## Mission
Generate qualified customer enquiries and professional sign-ups in Morocco using organic distribution first, with every content asset tied to a measurable landing page and CTA.

## Primary audiences
1. Customers: people who need a plumber, electrician, locksmith, painter, mason, tiler, carpenter, AC technician, cleaner, gardener or welder.
2. Professionals: Moroccan artisans and service providers looking for new customers.

## Priority channels
- Facebook: local/community reach and high-intent group distribution.
- Instagram: Reels + carousels for discovery and trust.
- TikTok: short practical home-service videos.
- YouTube Shorts: searchable short-form advice.
- Google Search Console/SEO: evergreen high-intent landing pages and guides.
- Google Business Profile: brand/entity discovery; Morocco is supported by Google Business Profile.

## Content pillars
### Customer education
- How to choose a professional before paying.
- Questions to ask before a quote.
- Red flags for home-service scams.
- What information to send when requesting a quote.
- Emergency vs planned work.

### Morocco-specific practical advice
- Casablanca/Rabat/Marrakech/Tangier/Agadir/Fes/Oujda/Kenitra examples.
- French + Darija phrasing where natural.
- Seasonal home-maintenance topics.

### Marketplace utility
- How Snay3i search works.
- How to compare profiles.
- How to contact a professional.
- How professionals can create/use a profile.

### Professional acquisition
- Why a complete profile matters.
- How to photograph work professionally.
- How to write a clear service description.
- What customers ask before hiring.

## 30-day cadence
### Every day
- 1 short-form video concept.
- 1 supporting text post/caption.
- 1 CTA to one relevant Snay3i page.
- Record content URL, source, date, clicks and leads.

### Weekly themes
Week 1 — Plomberie
Week 2 — Electricité
Week 3 — Serrurerie / sécurité maison
Week 4 — Rénovation / peinture / carrelage

## Conversion rules
Every customer post must end with one CTA, never several competing CTAs.
Use relevant destination pages, not the homepage, whenever a service/city page exists.
Primary CTA: “Trouvez des professionnels sur Snay3i.ma”.
Professional CTA: “Ajoutez votre activité sur Snay3i.ma”.

## Measurement
Track:
- visits by source,
- service/city landing-page visits,
- profile views,
- phone/WhatsApp/contact clicks,
- professional sign-ups,
- enquiries per channel,
- enquiries per city/service,
- repeat visitors.

Do not optimize for likes alone. Optimize for qualified enquiries and professional registrations.

## Free-growth priority
1. Facebook local/community distribution.
2. Instagram Reels.
3. TikTok.
4. YouTube Shorts.
5. Google organic/SEO.
6. Google Business Profile.

## Guardrails
- No fabricated statistics.
- No “verified” or “guaranteed” claims unless Snay3i can substantiate them.
- No spam posting in groups.
- No fake reviews.
- No paid promotion until an organic conversion baseline exists.

## Operating model
Strategy, content briefs, scripts, SEO priorities, landing-page recommendations and reporting are owned centrally. Actual publishing requires connected access to the relevant social accounts; until a publishing integration is connected, prepared assets should be posted manually or via the account owner’s scheduler.
