# AdSense-safe growth guardrails

- Keep the submitted AdSense application active; do not repeatedly resubmit.
- Preserve privacy, terms, contact, consent, ads.txt and publisher configuration.
- Prefer useful original content over page-count expansion.
- Avoid doorway pages and near-duplicate city/service pages.
- Never fabricate reviews, ratings, prices, certifications, verification status, availability or guarantees.
- Make marketplace claims match the actual product experience.
- Keep navigation clear and customer-focused.
- Measure real enquiries rather than vanity traffic.
