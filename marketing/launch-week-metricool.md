## Rules
- Publish on Facebook and TikTok only for this launch.
- Do not claim that artisans are verified unless the profile data actually supports it.
- Do not invent reviews, ratings, availability, customer counts or guarantees.
- Keep the AdSense submission untouched.
- Review conversions after the first 7 days: profile views, registration starts, registrations, phone clicks, WhatsApp clicks and lead conversions.
