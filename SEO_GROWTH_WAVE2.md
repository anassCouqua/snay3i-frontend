# Snay3i SEO & Conversion Wave 2

## Objective
Turn organic search traffic into real customer enquiries while keeping AdSense-safe quality standards.

## Priority service clusters
1. Plomberie
2. Électricité
3. Serrurerie
4. Peinture
5. Maçonnerie
6. Climatisation
7. Carrelage
8. Menuiserie

## Priority cities
Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech, Fès, Tanger, Agadir, Meknès, Oujda.

## Publishing rule
Only publish a city/service page when it contains genuinely useful local information, clear service scope, customer guidance, and a meaningful path to real professionals. Never generate near-identical doorway pages.

## Conversion requirements
Every landing page should provide:
- clear service/city heading
- practical customer guidance
- prominent route to find professionals
- relevant internal links
- transparent expectations around pricing and availability
- no unsupported verification, ratings, reviews, or guarantees

## Artisan funnel
Create a clear professional CTA and onboarding path. The proposition should explain that Snay3i helps professionals become discoverable by customers; avoid promising guaranteed leads or earnings.

## Measurement
Track, where technically available and privacy-compliantly configured:
- landing-page visits
- service searches
- professional/profile views
- contact/phone/WhatsApp clicks
- registration starts
- completed artisan profiles

## AdSense guardrail
Do not alter the submitted AdSense application. Improve the site's actual user value, trust, navigation, and content quality without adding ads or thin pages merely to increase page count.
